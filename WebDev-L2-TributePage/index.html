About Nimsdai

Nirmal Purja, known as Nimsdai, was a Nepalese mountaineer and former British Special Forces soldier. He became famous for pushing the limits of high-altitude mountaineering.

His greatest achievement was Project Possible, through which he climbed all fourteen of the world's 8,000-metre peaks in just 6 months and 6 days in 2019.

His expeditions showed extraordinary physical strength, preparation and teamwork. He was also recognised for helping fellow climbers during dangerous situations.

His story continues to inspire people to challenge limitations and approach difficult goals with a determined mindset.

Key Achievements
